01 — Signature

Toy Oshi

Festive Plov · Wedding Ceremonial

The crown jewel of Uzbek gastronomy. Reserved for celebrations of life's most sacred moments.

Slow-cooked over firewood for 12 hours in a cast-iron kazan inherited through generations. Premium Devzira rice from Fergana Valley, hand-selected lamb, golden carrots, and a crown of whole garlic heads. The fat renders until it sings. A dish that cannot be rushed — and never should be.

Signature · Seasonal
02 — Daily

Choyxona Oshi

Teahouse Plov · Daily Ritual

The soul of Uzbek communal dining. An everyday pilgrimage that locals return to, again and again.

Prepared fresh each morning before the sun rises. The aroma of cumin, barberries, and rendered lamb fat drifts through the neighborhood like an ancient invitation. Served piping hot, communally, from a single vast kazan. Every bowl carries the warmth of shared history.

Daily · Morning
03 — Rare

Devzira Special

Heritage Rice · Chef's Reserve

Crafted exclusively with Fergana Valley's most prized grain — a rice so rare it borders on mythological.

Red Devzira rice absorbs fat and broth with extraordinary capacity, yielding a texture and depth unmatched by any other variety. Each grain swells with the essence of fire, spice, and patience. Available in limited quantity. This is not simply plov — it is an argument for slowing down.

Limited · Premium
04 — Accompaniment

Shakarob Salad

Tomato & Onion · Traditional

Deceptive in simplicity, extraordinary in purpose. The counterpoint that every master plov demands.

Garden-fresh tomatoes sliced paper-thin, sweet white onions dressed in pure pomegranate vinegar and black pepper — nothing more. But the acidity and freshness cut through the richness of plov with surgical precision. In Uzbek culinary philosophy, the shakarob is not a side dish; it is a necessity.

Fresh · Traditional
The Experience

More than a meal

The Living Fire

Our kazan has not been extinguished in 27 years. Fueled by fruit-wood, tended by hands that know the language of flame — it is the heart of everything we serve.

Time as Ingredient

Patience is our most guarded recipe. Nothing here is accelerated, optimized, or compromised. Twelve hours of slow cooking is not a method — it is a philosophy of respect.

Communal Grace

In our tradition, food is never eaten alone in spirit. The table is a gathering of souls. We provide the kazan; you provide the company.
